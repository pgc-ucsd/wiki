## Privacy

This repository is **public**, and has to be — GitHub's free plan only serves
Pages from public repos. Everything committed here is world-readable and stays
in the git history forever, even after deletion.

!!! danger "Never commit"
    - Member contact lists or rosters
    - Financial records, budgets tied to named people
    - Anything shared with you in confidence
    - Credentials of any kind

## Contributing

Corrections are welcome from anyone. Click the pencil icon at the top right of
