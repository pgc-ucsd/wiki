# About PGC

## What PGC is

The Physics Graduate Council (PGC) is a student organization that's meant to act as an intermediary between the students and the department. We work to provide support to the student body for a range of issues, from coursework to research to TAing.

PGC also organizes social gatherings to build community amongst the grad students.

## Privacy

This repository is **public**, and has to be — GitHub's free plan only serves
Pages from public repos. Everything committed here is world-readable and stays
in the git history forever, even after deletion.

!!! danger "Never commit"
    - Member contact lists or rosters
    - Financial records, budgets tied to named people
    - Anything shared with you in confidence
    - Credentials of any kind
