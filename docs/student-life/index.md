# Student Life

Living in San Diego and being a person outside the department.

!!! note "The official version is elsewhere"
    Formal services — health, insurance, financial aid, campus offices — are in
    [Resources](../resources/index.md). This section is for the informal side,
    which is where a student-run wiki is actually more useful than a handbook.

## Housing

Grad and family housing is run through [**HDH**](https://hdhgradfamilyhousing.ucsd.edu/apply/index.html). Dormitory accommodation is reserved for undergraduates, so graduate students are looking at campus apartments or the private market. See [Housing](housing.md) for more info about applying to grad housing or living off-campus.

## Getting around

Free campus shuttle, the Triton U-Pass for regional transit, and parking
options are on [Transportation](transportation.md).

## Community

Tea time
:   Graduate students run a **weekly tea time** during the academic year, with
    coffee, tea, and cookies. Weekly emails announce the time and location. It
    is an informal chance to talk physics with faculty, research staff, and
    other students — and one of the easier ways to meet people outside your
    cohort.

Colloquium
:   **PHYS 260**, Thursdays at 4:00 p.m. Everyone is expected to attend, and it
    doubles as a weekly fixed point in the social calendar.

PGC
:   The Physics Graduate Council represents graduate students in the
    department. Reps introduce themselves at orientation. See
    [About PGC](../about/index.md).

Discord
:   The fastest way to get a question answered — the invite is on the
    [home page](../index.md).

GPSA
:   The campus-wide Graduate and Professional Student Association.

Identity and community centers
:   The Cross-Cultural Center, LGBT Resource Center, Women's Center, and others
    are listed under [Campus Services](../resources/campus-services.md).

## Cost of living

!!! danger "The September-to-November gap"
    Your first fall paycheck arrives **November 1**, but you arrive in
    September. You need to cover roughly six weeks of rent, deposits, and
    living costs before any money appears. This is the single most common
    financial surprise for first-years.

An honest picture of what a stipend covers in San Diego belongs here. Keep it
factual, and **date every figure**.

!!! warning "Keep it general"
    Do not put individual stipend amounts, offer details, or the finances of
    named people in this repository — it is public and permanent. General
    ranges and published pay scales are fine. See
    [About PGC](../about/index.md#privacy).
