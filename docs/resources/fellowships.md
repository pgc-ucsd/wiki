# External Fellowships

Fellowships from outside UCSD, split by citizenship requirement. More are
listed on the **GEPA fellowships page**.

!!! warning "Verify before you apply"
    Amounts, eligibility, and deadlines change every cycle. These entries come
    from the department's orientation handbook and are a starting point for
    searching, not an application guide. Always check the program's own site.

!!! tip "Apply early in your program"
    Several of these are explicitly aimed at students **at or near the
    beginning** of doctoral study, and become ineligible after one or two years.
    If you are a first- or second-year, this is the window.

## US citizens and permanent residents

### Hertz Graduate Fellowship

**Up to $250,000** — cost-of-education allowance plus a personal stipend.

Applicants must be college seniors, first-year graduate students, or in a gap
period preparing to apply; intend to pursue a PhD in the applied physical or
biological sciences, mathematics, or engineering; intend to direct their studies
toward understanding and solving major near-term problems facing society; and be
US citizens or permanent residents.

Fannie and John Hertz Foundation · (925) 373-1642 ·
[hertzfoundation.org](https://www.hertzfoundation.org/) ·
fellowshipinfo@hertzfoundation.org

### SMART Scholarship Program

**$30,000–$46,000** stipend depending on degree, plus full tuition and
education-related fees and summer internship opportunities.

Requires eligibility to hold a security clearance, participation in summer
internships at Department of Defense laboratories, and willingness to accept
**post-graduate employment with the DoD**. Minimum 3.0 cumulative GPA.

Scholarship-for-Service Program · (571) 633-7940 ·
[smartscholarship.org](https://www.smartscholarship.org/) ·
smart@smartscholarship.com

### National Defense Science and Engineering Graduate (NDSEG) Fellowship

**Full tuition and fees for up to four years**, a monthly stipend, and up to
$3,600 in medical insurance. **No military or service obligation.**

For students at or near the beginning of doctoral study in STEM. You must have
received or be on track to receive your bachelor's by the end of the academic
year, **or** have completed less than two full-time years of graduate study in
the discipline. Requires a research proposal relevant to DoD and at least three
recommendation letters.

DoD NDSEG Fellowship Program Office · (301) 948-4232 ·
NDSEG.PMO@sysplus.com

### Stewardship Science Graduate Fellowship

**$45,000.**

For full-time students in disciplines related to **high energy density physics,
nuclear science, or materials under extreme conditions and hydrodynamics**.
Summers are spent on full-time research, coursework, or a practicum at a DOE
NNSA laboratory. **Your department must waive teaching requirements beyond one
term.**

Krell Institute · (515) 956-3696 · [krellinst.org/ssgf](https://www.krellinst.org/ssgf/)

### Dr. Nancy Foster Scholarship Program

**Up to $47,000** — a $35,000 twelve-month stipend plus up to $12,000
education allowance, and up to $10,000 for a 4–6 week collaboration at a NOAA
facility. Renewable.

For students pursuing a master's or doctorate in **oceanography, maritime
archaeology, or marine biology**, with a 3.3 GPA or above. Doctoral scholars
must complete at least one program collaboration. Women and minority students
are particularly encouraged to apply.

NOAA Office of National Marine Sanctuaries · (831) 647-4204 ·
[fosterscholars.noaa.gov](https://fosterscholars.noaa.gov/) ·
fosterscholars@noaa.gov

## No citizenship requirement

### Amelia Earhart Fellowship

**$10,000.**

Women of any nationality pursuing a PhD in **aerospace-related studies**.
Full-time, with **more than two years of graduate school remaining**. Three
recommendation letters — one must be from a thesis supervisor or advisor, one
from a university professor.

Zonta International · (630) 928-1400 · programs@zonta.org

### Faculty for the Future

**Up to $50,000** for PhD study (up to $40,000 for postdoctoral research).
Childcare grants may also be awarded.

For **women who are citizens of a developing country or emerging economy**, as
defined chiefly by the UN Human Development Index. Fellows are expected to
return to their home countries after completing their studies to strengthen
STEM teaching and research there, or to pursue public-sector positions applying
their skills to STEM policy.

faculty4future@slb.com

### AAUW International Fellowships

**$20,000–$50,000.**

Open only to women who are **not** US citizens or permanent residents. Requires
the equivalent of a US bachelor's degree by September 30 of the application
year, and having already applied to your proposed institution. Applicants must
intend to return to their home country after graduation. **$30 nonrefundable
filing fee.** Preference goes to applicants showing prior commitment to the
advancement of women and girls.

(202) 785-7700 · aauw@act.org

### SEG Scholarships

**$500–$10,000**, averaging around $3,200.

Open to graduate and undergraduate students studying **geophysics or a closely
related field**, with above-average grades. Two faculty recommendation letters.

scholarships@seg.org

### ORISE Research Internship Program

**Amount varies.**

For graduate students in good standing at an accredited institution, or
post-baccalaureates within one year of graduation, with a minimum **2.5 GPA**
and an interest in **energy-related technologies**.

experienceORISE@orau.org
