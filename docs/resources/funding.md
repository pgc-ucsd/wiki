# Funding and Fees

Where the money comes from, what you owe, and what employment covers.

!!! warning "Dollar figures go stale fast"
    The amounts on this page come from the department's **2025–26** orientation
    handbook and are recorded to give you a sense of scale. Fees and tuition
    are reset annually — confirm current figures with the
    [Registrar](https://students.ucsd.edu/finances/fees/) or the Grad Financial
    Support Coordinator before relying on them.

## Assistantships

Graduate students in good academic standing, enrolled full-time (12+ units),
may be employed as **Teaching Assistants** or **Graduate Student Researchers**
up to **50% time — 20 hours per week** — during the academic year.

New students admitted with financial support are usually appointed as **TAs for
one academic year**, from the start of fall quarter in September to the end of
spring quarter in June.

After the first year, students are encouraged to affiliate with a research
group and be supported as a **GSR**. Ask the faculty member you want to work
with **well in advance** — spring quarter for the following year. GSR funding
comes from federal contracts and grants, so it depends on funds being available
that year. Some TA positions remain open to advanced students.

Fellowship recipients may also hold part-time TA or GSR appointments.

See [Teaching](../teaching/index.md) and [Research](../research/index.md).

## What employment covers

!!! tip "The 25% rule"
    Students supported as **GSRs or Academic Student Employees (TAs and
    graders) at 25% time or more** during a quarter have their **tuition, fees,
    and health insurance paid for that quarter**.

Students awarded TAships or University Fellowships receive full or partial fee
and tuition scholarships — the details are in your letter of support. Scholarship
payments arrive as an electronic credit on your fee statement once you enroll.

## If you are not supported

Unsupported students pay, per the 2025–26 handbook:

| | Amount (2025–26) |
| --- | --- |
| Registration, educational, and health insurance fees | **$21,375** per year (resident and non-resident) |
| Non-resident tuition, additionally | **$15,102** per year |

## California residency

US citizens and permanent residents can usually establish California residency
after living in the state for **one year**, which removes the non-resident
tuition charge.

To qualify as a resident for tuition purposes you must have established
residence in California for **more than one year immediately preceding** the
residence determination date for the term, **and** have relinquished your
previous residence.

Contact the Registrar's Office **as soon as you arrive on campus** — the clock
matters. Details:
[Residency for tuition purposes](https://students.ucsd.edu/finances/fees/residence/index.html).

## Tuition scholarships

The department holds a small number of tuition scholarships for **incoming
non-resident students** who meet a standard of outstanding scholarship.

Partial tuition scholarships are sometimes available for **continuing
non-resident students** who maintain a **3.5 GPA in core Physics courses** and
meet other departmental criteria.

## Summer employment

Part-time or full-time summer research assistantships are often available
within the department **after your first year**, subject to federal funding.
Arrange these **directly with the professor** running the project.

There are also summer session TA and tutor positions. Direct inquiries to the
**Student Affairs Manager beginning in April** — see
[Department Directory](directory.md).

## Student loans

The University administers a loan program for students demonstrating financial
need, through the **Financial Aid Office**. Ask for the Financial Aid Timeline
for Current Graduate Students.

## Visa financial certification

Before UCSD issues visa documents, international students must certify they
have sufficient funds to cover all fees, transportation, and living expenses
for their first year.

Per the 2025–26 handbook, the minimum was **$70,813** for the year.
