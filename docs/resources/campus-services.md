# Campus Services

An A–Z of campus offices and services worth knowing exist, from the
department's orientation handbook.

!!! tip "Most of these you will need exactly once"
    Skim it now so you remember it exists later. Health and insurance have
    their own page — see [Health and Insurance](health.md).

## Academics and records

Office of the Registrar
:   Enrollment, transcripts, residency classification.

Residency requirements and change of classification
:   See [Funding and Fees](funding.md#california-residency).

Graduate Division (GEPA)
:   The Division of Graduate Education and Postdoctoral Affairs. Owns leave of
    absence, in absentia, half-time study, candidacy paperwork, and the
    fellowships listing. Deadlines are in
    [Academic Deadlines](../coursework/deadlines.md).

Library information
:   Also where the copy machines for personal use live — departmental copiers
    are for official use only.

## Money

Financial Aid and Scholarship Office
:   The University administers a **loan program** for students demonstrating
    financial need. See the Financial Aid Timeline for Current Graduate
    Students.

Triton Printing · Post Office
:   Campus printing and mail services.

## Identity and access

Campus ID Card
:   Submit your photo through the **Online Photo Tool** before you arrive. The
    department collects IDs from the Campus Cards office and distributes them
    at orientation, coded with Mayer Hall access.

IT Service Portal
:   Information technology support. Campus wifi setup instructions live on
    Blink.

Social Security Number for international students
:   See [Pay and Payroll](payroll.md#social-security-number).

## Health and wellbeing

Counseling and Psychological Services (CAPS)
:   Mental health support. The **24-hour crisis line is 619-236-3339** — see
    [Department Directory](directory.md) for emergency numbers.

Health Services and Health Insurance
:   See [Health and Insurance](health.md).

Child Care
:   Campus childcare services.

## Safety and legal

OPHD
:   Office for the Prevention of Harassment and Discrimination. Covered during
    orientation Day 2.

Safety Awareness and Sexual Assault Resource Center
:   Support and reporting.

Student Legal Services
:   Free legal advice for students.

## Getting around and living

Transportation Services
:   Parking, shuttles, and transit passes — see
    [Transportation](../student-life/transportation.md).

Grad Housing
:   HDH Grad and Family Housing. Dormitories are reserved for undergraduates.
    See [Student Life](../student-life/index.md#housing).

## Careers and teaching

Career Center
:   Job search and career development.

Teaching + Learning Commons
:   Teaching workshops, the English Language Program for International
    Instructors, and the Writing + Critical Expression Hub. See
    [Teaching](../teaching/index.md#training-and-support).
