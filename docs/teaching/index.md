# Teaching

Nearly every first-year TAs. This section covers the appointment, the job, and
what is expected of you.

!!! note "It is a degree requirement"
    Every graduate student must complete **at least one quarter of Teaching
    Assistantship**, lecture or lab. Students satisfying it by teaching at UCSD
    enroll in **PHYS 500 in the fall of their first year** — mandatory, and
    only offered in fall. See
    [PhD Requirements](../coursework/phd-requirements.md#teaching-requirement).

## Getting an appointment

If a TA position was part of your support package, **you learn your assignment
at the department orientation in September**, during the TA briefing. There is
also a [TA application](https://physics.ucsd.edu/students/graduate/ta-application)
for positions beyond that.

New students admitted with financial support are usually appointed as TAs for
one full academic year, from the start of fall quarter in September to the end
of spring in June. Appointments run up to **50% time — 20 hours per week** —
and require full-time enrollment (12+ units) and good academic standing.

You may serve as **both a lecture TA and a lab TA** over your first year.

International students whose undergraduate degree was not taught in English
must certify their English proficiency before they can TA — see
[English Certification for International TAs](elce.md). Without it, employment
is limited to lower-paying grader positions.

## The three roles

| Role | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| **Teaching Assistant** | Preparing and running discussion or lab sections, proctoring, grading, office hours, overseeing graders and proctors, assisting with course administration |
| **Grader** | Mostly grading quizzes and exams, and proctoring |
| **Graduate Student Researcher** | Duties assigned by the faculty member running the funded project |

## Pay

!!! warning "Your first fall paycheck arrives November 1"
    Quarterly TA salary is split into **three equal paychecks**. Fall quarter
    starts in September, but the first check is issued **November 1** — roughly
    six weeks after you arrive. Budget for that gap.

Set up direct deposit immediately, or your check gets mailed to the home
address on your employment paperwork. See
[Pay and Payroll](../resources/payroll.md).

Current salary rates are published by GEPA each year; ask the Grad Financial
Support and TA Coordinator for the current scale — see the
[Department Directory](../resources/directory.md).

TAs and GSRs at **25% time or more** have tuition, fees, and health insurance
covered for that quarter. See [Funding and Fees](../resources/funding.md).

## Reporting your time

All UAW-represented employees — GSRs, ASEs, postdocs, some academic researchers
— **must report time in Ecotime** each pay period, even when there is nothing
unusual to report. See [Pay and Payroll](../resources/payroll.md#ecotime).

## Training and support

The department partners with the **Teaching + Learning Commons**, which assigns
workshops to help you prepare. A list of available workshops is shared once
determined. The Commons also runs a weekly teaching and learning workshop
series open to graduate students, postdocs, and faculty, plus a **Writing +
Critical Expression Hub** offering one-on-one writing tutoring, graduate writing
retreats, and workshops on graduate-level writing.

Lab TAs for the Physics 1-series get dedicated **lab TA training**, usually
around the day before instruction begins.

## Your rights

TAs and GSRs at UCSD are represented by the **UAW**. See [Union](../resources/union.md) for more details about the union, its goals, when issues warrant involving them, how to get involved, timeline for contract negotiations, and contact info.
