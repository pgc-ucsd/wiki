# Advanced Graduate Courses

You need **five** of these, from **at least three different groups**, with a
grade of **C or better** and a **3.0 average across four of the five**,
completed no later than the **end of your third year**.

Electives taken to satisfy the [core requirement](phd-requirements.md#core-courses-and-electives)
may also count here.

!!! tip "Alternative route"
    Instead of the course requirement, you may petition to take an **oral
    examination covering three areas of physics**.

## Biophysics students

Biophysics PhD students select five courses from **Biology, Biochemistry,
Chemistry, or Physics**, chosen in consultation with their adviser. At least
**three must be graduate courses**, and any Physics courses come from the eight
groups above.

## Course checklist

The department distributes a printable checklist covering the first-year grid
and every advanced course above, with a box per course, plus checkboxes for
PHYS 500 and the one-quarter TA requirement. Ask the Graduate Student
Coordinator for the current version — see the
[Department Directory](../resources/directory.md).
