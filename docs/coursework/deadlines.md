# Academic Deadlines

All academic deadlines are set by the Office of the Registrar and are subject
to change. The ones below are **specific to graduate students and identical in
all three quarters** — which makes them worth memorizing once.

!!! tip "Count Fridays, not dates"
    These deadlines are defined by *week of the quarter*, not calendar date, so
    they hold from year to year. For the actual dates in a given term, see the
    [Registrar's Enrollment and Registration Calendar](https://students.ucsd.edu/academics/enroll/calendars/index.html).

## The recurring pattern

| Deadline | What is due |
| --- | --- |
| **2nd Friday** | Half-time study requests · Leave of absence · In absentia requests · Applications to candidacy for the Master's degree (all via the **GEPA tool**) |
| **4th Friday** | Add/drop/change via **TEA** — change a grading option, change units, or drop a course **without a W** |
| **9th Friday** | Drop a course via **TEA** |
| **10th Friday** | Add a course via **TEA** |
| **11th Friday** (finals week) | Final paperwork for degree completion |

_TEA is the new university-wide system used to request units for 298 and 299. This replaced EASy in 2026._

!!! warning "Department approval is not the deadline"
    Fully completed forms must be **received by GEPA** by the deadline.
    Departmental approval alone does not count as meeting it.

To receive the Master's in the same quarter you apply, the candidacy
application must land by the **2nd Friday**.

## Degree completion

**Spring degree.** To have your name printed in the commencement booklet, all
final paperwork is due the **Friday of 10th week**. The absolute final deadline
is the **last Friday of spring quarter**, but a submission that late will not
get your name in the booklet.

**Summer degree.** The last Friday of summer session II — usually early
September.

## Shape of the academic year

Exact dates change annually, but the rhythm does not:

| Quarter | Instruction begins | Instruction ends |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Fall** | Late September | Early December, finals following |
| **Winter** | Early January | Mid-March, finals following |
| **Spring** | Late March | Early-to-mid June, finals following |

Each quarter runs roughly **48–49 days of instruction**. Commencement follows
the end of spring quarter.

Holidays observed during the academic year include Veterans Day, Thanksgiving,
winter break and New Year, Martin Luther King Jr. Day, Presidents' Day, Cesar
Chavez Day, and Memorial Day.

For the authoritative version, see the
[UCSD academic calendar](https://blink.ucsd.edu/instructors/resources/academic/calendars/index.html).

## Enrollment deadline for incoming students

Separate from the above, and the one that catches new students: the deadline to
enroll and pay fees falls **before instruction begins** in fall. Missing it
costs **$100** in late fees. See [Enrollment](../onboarding/enrollment.md).
