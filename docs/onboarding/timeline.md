# Quarter-by-Quarter Timeline

The first year, in order, with the deadlines that actually cost you something
if you miss them.

!!! warning "Dates shift every year"
    Exact calendar dates change annually and are set by the Office of the
    Registrar. What is stable is the **pattern** — which week of the quarter a
    deadline falls in, and roughly when in the month things happen. Always
    confirm against the
    [Registrar's Enrollment and Registration Calendar](https://students.ucsd.edu/academics/enroll/calendars/index.html)
    and the department's emailed orientation schedule.

## Before fall quarter

| When | What |
| --- | --- |
| As soon as you have a PID | Enroll in fall classes — [Enrollment](enrollment.md) |
| Late August to mid-September | ELCE for international students — [ELCE](../teaching/elce.md) |
| Before instruction begins | **Enrollment deadline.** Missing it costs $100 in late fees |
| Mid-September | Diagnostic exam, then advisor meeting |
| Mid-September | Two mandatory days of [orientation](orientation.md) |
| Day before instruction, roughly | Lab TA training for Physics 1-series TAs |

## Fall

**Courses:** 200A (Theoretical Mechanics), 201 (Mathematical Physics), 212A
(Quantum Mechanics), plus **PHYS 500** — mandatory, first-year, fall only.

| When | What |
| --- | --- |
| Late September | Instruction begins |
| **2nd Friday** | Deadline for half-time study, leave of absence, in absentia, and Master's candidacy applications (all via the GEPA tool) |
| **4th Friday** | Deadline to change grading option or units, or drop without a W (via EASy) |
| **9th Friday** | Deadline to drop a course (via EASy) |
| **10th Friday** | Deadline to add a course (via EASy) |
| **November 1** | **First fall paycheck.** Quarterly TA salary is paid in three equal installments — the fall quarter starts in September but the first check does not arrive until November 1. Budget for this gap. |
| Early-to-mid December | Instruction ends, then finals |

!!! danger "Plan for the November 1 paycheck"
    This catches nearly every first-year out. You arrive in September and are
    not paid until November. Make sure you can cover roughly six weeks of rent
    and living costs before your first check clears, and enroll in
    [direct deposit](../resources/payroll.md) immediately so it is not delayed
    further.

## Winter

**Courses:** 203A (Advanced Classical Electrodynamics), 212B (Quantum
Mechanics), plus an elective.

| When | What |
| --- | --- |
| Early January | Instruction begins |
| Throughout | **PHYS 261**, the research seminar introducing UCSD physics groups, runs weekly this quarter. Go — this is how you meet potential advisors. |
| Before winter employment | Payroll direct deposit must be set up — [Pay and Payroll](../resources/payroll.md) |
| 2nd / 4th / 9th / 10th Friday | Same recurring deadlines as fall |
| Mid-March | Instruction ends, then finals |

## Spring

**Courses:** 203B (Advanced Classical Electrodynamics), 210A (Equilibrium
Statistical Mechanics), plus an elective.

| When | What |
| --- | --- |
| Late March | Instruction begins |
| April onward | Summer research positions get arranged. Ask professors directly; inquiries about summer TA or tutor work go to the Student Affairs Manager |
| Throughout spring | The department submits its **annual evaluation** of every student to GEPA — [Annual Evaluation](../research/annual-evaluation.md) |
| 2nd / 4th / 9th / 10th Friday | Same recurring deadlines |
| Early-to-mid June | Instruction ends, then finals |
| **June 30** | **Written progress report due to your advisor** — max two pages. [Annual Evaluation](../research/annual-evaluation.md) |

## Summer

- **Begin research.** This is the expected point to start, if you have not
  already.
- The **Qualification Committee reviews your first-year coursework** and
  recommends corrective measures for anyone who has not met the grade
  standards.
- Summer research assistantships are often available after the first year,
  subject to federal funding. Arrange these directly with a professor.

## Beyond the first year

| Year | What is expected |
| --- | --- |
| **Second** | Finish any remaining core courses and electives. Begin advanced graduate courses. **Secure a research advisor by the end of spring quarter.** |
| **Third** | Conduct research. Complete course requirements and **advance to candidacy by the end of the year.** |
| **Fourth and beyond** | Research, write the thesis, defend before your PhD committee. |

Details are in [PhD Requirements](../coursework/phd-requirements.md) and
[Research](../research/index.md).

## Recurring deadlines

The quarterly add/drop pattern above is identical in all three quarters. It is
written out in full, with the finals-week and degree-completion deadlines, in
[Academic Deadlines](../coursework/deadlines.md).
