    Advanced Graduate Courses¶

    You need five of these, from at least three different groups, with a grade of C or better and a 3.0 average across four of the five, completed no later than the end of your third year.

    Electives taken to satisfy the core requirement may also count here.

    Alternative route

    Instead of the course requirement, you may petition to take an oral examination covering three areas of physics.

    Group 1 — Plasma¶

    Course Title
    PHYS 218A / 218B / 218C Plasma Physics
    PHYS 235 Nonlinear Plasma Theory

    Group 2 — Condensed Matter¶

    Course Title
    PHYS 211A / 211B Solid State Physics
    PHYS 219 Condensed Matter Laboratory
    PHYS 230 Advanced Solid State
    PHYS 232 Electronic Materials

    Group 3 — Particle Physics / High Energy¶

    Course Title
    PHYS 214 Elementary Particles
    PHYS 215A / 215B / 215C Particles and Fields
    PHYS 222A Experimental Techniques in Physics

    Group 4 — Mathematics¶

    Course Title
    PHYS 210B Nonequilibrium Statistical Mechanics
    PHYS 221A Advanced Mechanics
    PHYS 243 Stochastic Methods
    MATH 210A / 210B / 210C Mathematical Physics
    MATH 259A / 259B / 259C Geometrical Physics

    The course checklist also lists PHYS 244 under this group.

    Group 5 — Biophysics¶

    Course Title
    PHYS 273 Biological Information
    PHYS 274 Quantitative Biology: Stochastic Population Genetics
    PHYS 275 Fundamentals of Biological Physics
    PHYS 276 Quantitative Molecular Biology
    PHYS 277 Physics of the Cell
    PHYS 278 Biophysics of Neurons

    Group 6 — Astrophysics¶

    Course Title
    PHYS 223 Stellar Structure
    PHYS 224 Interstellar Medium
    PHYS 226 Galaxies and Galactic Dynamics
    PHYS 227 Cosmology
    PHYS 228 High Energy Astrophysics and Compact Objects
    PHYS 238 Observational Astronomy Laboratory

    Group 7 — General¶

    Course Title
    PHYS 217 Renormalization Field Theory
    PHYS 220 Group Theory
    PHYS 225A / 225B Relativity

    The course checklist also lists PHYS 216 in this group.

    Group 8 — Computational¶

    Course Title
    PHYS 241 / 242 Computational Physics
    PHYS 244 Parallel Computing

    Biophysics students¶

    Biophysics PhD students select five courses from Biology, Biochemistry, Chemistry, or Physics, chosen in consultation with their adviser. At least three must be graduate courses, and any Physics courses come from the eight groups above.

    Course checklist¶

    The department distributes a printable checklist covering the first-year grid and every advanced course above, with a box per course, plus checkboxes for PHYS 500 and the one-quarter TA requirement. Ask the Graduate Student Coordinator for the current version — see the Department Directory.
